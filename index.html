VLSRB / WACV
IEEE/CVF WACV 2026 Main Conference Pages 5754–5765

Enhancing Vision Language Corruption Robustness using Cross-Distribution & Prompted Denoisers

Sameer Shafayet Latif, Sadab Shiper, K. M. Rahiduzzaman Kiran, Md Farhan Ishmam, Md Azam Hossain, Abu Raihan Mostofa Kamal, Md Hamjajul Ashmafee
Introduces the VLSRB benchmark spanning 36 multimodal corruptions and a modular MoE-inspired routing denoiser yielding up to 5.5% overall accuracy gains.
PDF (CVF) DOI Code (GitHub) Poster Video
Abstract Modern Vision-Language Models (VLMs) suffer significant performance degradation under real-world corruptions. We introduce the Vision-Language System Robustness Benchmark (VLSRB) covering 36 multimodal corruptions (18 visual and 18 textual perturbations) to evaluate VLM resilience under challenging distributions. To mitigate these corruptions without costly retraining of foundational backbones, we propose a modular dual-stage framework integrating Corruption-Specific Visual Denoisers (CSVD) routed via a lightweight Visual Corruption Routing Network (VCRN) paired with a zero-shot prompt-guided LLM textual denoiser. Our approach achieves up to 5.5% overall accuracy improvements and up to 9% gains across targeted VQA benchmarks.
